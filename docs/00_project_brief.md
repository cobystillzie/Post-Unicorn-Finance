# Post-Unicorn Finance Project Brief

## Goal

Establish **Post-Unicorn Finance** as a serious emerging industry category.

The argument is not that venture capital is bad. The argument is that venture capital is one asset class inside a broader innovation-finance universe. Many valuable companies, founders, investors, and liquidity paths are overlooked because they are not shaped for unicorn outcomes.

## First Artifact

The first artifact is a deep strategic research paper that combines:

- a financial taxonomy paper,
- a market map report,
- a category-defining thesis,
- and a source-backed evidence base.

The paper should withstand scrutiny from founders, VCs, PE investors, angels, HNW/accredited investors, accelerator directors, corporate innovation leaders, and strategic acquirers.

## Locked Direction

- Public category name: **Post-Unicorn Finance**.
- First paper audience: founders and investors.
- VC stance: complementary, not anti-VC.
- SMV expansion: **Strategic Middle Ventures**.
- Geography: global unrestricted.
- Sector scope: all tech-enabled companies.
- Data posture: evidence-first industry entity rows, with one source-backed claim per entity.
- Company examples and exits: supporting evidence for SMV and case-study chapters, not the first proof layer.
- Initial platform posture: market intelligence first, fund marketplace later and cautiously.
- Regulatory posture: no investment advice, no transaction claims, no marketplace readiness claims.
- Pegasus / Startup World Cup: strategic appendix only; do not imply endorsement.
- Liquidity / locked-out pain: supporting section, not the pinnacle of the paper.

## Thesis In One Sentence

Post-Unicorn Finance names, proves, and organizes the overlooked capital stack for valuable innovation companies that are not optimized for unicorn outcomes.

## What Must Be Proven

The paper has to prove that this is not just a theory. It must show:

- firms, funds, platforms, acquirers, studios, communities, instruments, and capital providers already operating around non-unicorn outcomes,
- industry infrastructure organized across SMV, Patient Capital, LMV, Sovereign Capital, Search/ETA, Portfolio Capital, and related modifiers, with instruments tracked as financing mechanisms rather than entity asset classes,
- enough entities per lane to suggest a market map rather than isolated anecdotes,
- companies that built valuable outcomes without classic unicorn financing,
- strategic middle exits and acquisition pathways,
- investors and funds already organizing around non-unicorn models,
- instruments that already exist outside classic priced-equity VC,
- founder/investor discourse showing dissatisfaction with VC as the default standard,
- and enough market infrastructure to suggest an industry, not a handful of anecdotes.

## What Not To Do

- Do not claim Pegasus or Startup World Cup endorses the taxonomy.
- Do not make the paper primarily about illiquidity or being locked out.
- Do not claim a company was bootstrapped, profitable, or VC-free unless the source directly supports it.
- Do not claim an atlas row is a verified fact unless the source text has been reviewed.
- Do not call every category an asset class.
- Do not give investment advice.
- Do not imply the future fund marketplace is live, compliant, or inevitable.
