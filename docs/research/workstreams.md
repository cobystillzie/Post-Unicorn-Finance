# Workstreams

## 1. Industry Atlas Engine

Goal: build a broad global candidate database of the Post-Unicorn Finance industry itself.

Initial search targets:

- SMV capital providers and acquisition-first funds,
- profitable-growth and non-unicorn venture firms,
- patient-capital funds, evergreen vehicles, and holdcos,
- LMV, indie, solo-founder, and AI-native micro venture backers,
- search / ETA investors and communities,
- acquisition marketplaces and SMB/SaaS buying platforms,
- software acquirers and portfolio-capital platforms,
- sovereign-backed innovation funds and strategic public capital,
- revenue-based financing, royalties, shared earnings, capped-return, and milestone-tranche providers.

Output:

- raw entity leads,
- source-backed entity-claim rows,
- ranked industry-entity exports,
- per-asset-class market maps,
- curated proof set for the first paper chapter.

## 2. SMV Firm / Fund Proof

Goal: establish Strategic Middle Ventures as the first major proof lane inside the industry atlas.

For each entity, capture:

- entity name,
- role in the ecosystem,
- source URL,
- stated thesis or observable activity,
- asset bucket,
- active status,
- geography,
- instrument or model if visible,
- why it belongs in the atlas,
- what still needs verification.

## 3. Outcome + Company Proof

Goal: support the industry thesis with company histories and outcomes after the atlas exists.

For each company, capture:

- company name,
- event,
- source,
- amount or qualitative outcome,
- acquirer/investor,
- funding path,
- why this is Post-Unicorn Finance evidence,
- what still needs verification.

## 4. Instruments Map

Goal: show which instruments already exist and which entities use them.

Map:

- revenue-based financing,
- shared earnings,
- royalty financing,
- milestone tranches,
- capped returns,
- acquisition-first venture,
- search funds / ETA,
- acquisition marketplaces,
- venture studios,
- holding companies,
- SaaS acquirers,
- patient-capital funds.

## 5. Founder / Investor Discourse

Goal: collect language from people already talking around the category.

Search for:

- founders rejecting VC,
- founders delaying VC,
- bootstrapped founders explaining why,
- investors saying power-law VC does not fit most businesses,
- funds describing profitability-first or non-unicorn strategies.

Use this as supporting evidence and distribution strategy, not as the main proof.

## 6. Paper Architecture

Goal: turn the database into the paper.

The paper should follow an industry proof stack:

1. Thesis: VC is one asset class, not the whole system.
2. Taxonomy: define the Post-Unicorn Finance stack.
3. Industry atlas: firms, funds, platforms, acquirers, studios, providers, communities, and instruments.
4. SMV chapter: Strategic Middle Ventures as the first proof case.
5. Evidence: company outcomes and founder paths.
6. Liquidity chapter: private middle-company liquidity as a supporting pain point.
7. Pegasus/SWC appendix: strategic use case without endorsement claims.

## 7. Future Platform

Goal: preserve the research in a format that can become a product.

Near term:

- CSV plus Markdown.
- Strict schema.
- Validation script.
- Ranked exports.

Later:

- SQLite for local querying.
- Supabase for platform backend.
- Web UI for market intelligence.
- Optional regulated fund marketplace exploration only after legal review.
