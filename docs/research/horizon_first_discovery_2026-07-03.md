# Horizon-First Discovery Pass - 2026-07-03

Scope: discovery for Nimble and SMV candidates using explicit liquidity horizons only. Nimble = under 6 years. SMV = 7-9 years plus sub-unicorn / $50M-$500M outcome evidence where available. Revenue-based financing repayment periods were not treated as company-exit horizons.

Result: 6 rows were added directly to `data/evidence/atlas.csv`. The candidate bar was intentionally strict: a row needed both source-validity pass and taxonomy pass in the Santa-style adversarial review.
