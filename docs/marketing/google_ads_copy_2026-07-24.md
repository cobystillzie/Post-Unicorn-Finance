## What we're selling (so the copy stays honest)

The atlas is a **source-backed map of 165 capital allocators (across 28 countries) that fund companies OUTSIDE the ten-year, power-law "unicorn-or-bust" venture model.** Every entry is verified against the firm's own website and sorted by *liquidity horizon* — how (and how fast) capital comes back. It's free to browse, and it includes a 7-question founder quiz ("Should you pursue venture?").

**Who is searching for this**
- Founders who don't fit the unicorn mold and want alternative / non-dilutive funding.
- Operators researching revenue-based, evergreen, permanent, or steward-owned capital.
- LPs, allocators, and researchers tracking the emerging "post-unicorn" category.

**Our three strongest, defensible angles** (used throughout the copy):
1. **Alternative to unicorn VC** — a whole map of capital for the other 99% of companies.
2. **Credibility** — source-backed, verified in each firm's own words, 165 funds / 28 countries.
3. **Useful & free** — browse the atlas and take the founder-fit quiz, no login.

## Best-practice notes

- **Provide 15 headlines + 4 descriptions per RSA.** More assets = higher "Ad strength," which lifts impressions. The banks above give you plenty to fill every ad group without repeating.
- **Keep 2–3 headlines containing the ad group's exact keyword** so the ad reads as relevant to the search (that's why the themed table pairs keywords to headlines).
- **Don't over-pin.** Pinning locks Google out of optimizing. Pin only brand (H1) and a CTA (H3) if you must.
- **Add every callout and all four sitelinks** — extensions are free real estate and consistently lift CTR.
- **Mind the destination:** most CTAs point to the atlas home (`/`); the "Should you pursue venture?" quiz has its own page (`/should-you-pursue-venture/`) — send that traffic via the sitelink and the matching headline.
- **Honesty check:** the atlas is free and read-only (leads are source-verified, not directly editable). Avoid any "apply for funding" or "get funded" language — the site is a map, not a lender or broker.
