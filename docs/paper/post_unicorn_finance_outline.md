# Post-Unicorn Finance Paper Outline

## Working Title

**Post-Unicorn Finance: The Overlooked Capital Stack Around Venture Capital**

## Core Thesis

Venture capital is one asset class, not the whole innovation-finance universe. A large and growing set of valuable companies, founders, investors, instruments, and outcomes sit outside the unicorn path. Post-Unicorn Finance names and organizes that overlooked market.

## Abstract Draft

For decades, the startup ecosystem treated venture-backability as a proxy for innovation quality. That framing is now too narrow. Venture capital remains essential for power-law companies, but many technology-enabled businesses create substantial value through strategic acquisition, profitable compounding, delayed VC, patient capital, acquisition entrepreneurship, sovereign strategy, and AI-enabled micro-scale leverage. This paper introduces Post-Unicorn Finance as a framework for classifying those pathways and proving that the market already exists.

## Section 3: Industry Atlas

Purpose:

- Prove that Post-Unicorn Finance is already an industry by mapping the entities operating in it.
- Make firms, funds, platforms, acquirers, studios, communities, providers, and instruments the first proof layer.

Subclaims:

- The market already contains identifiable entities across multiple capital models.
- These entities do not all use the same language, so the paper's job is to organize the fragmented vocabulary.
- Company examples support the atlas, but the atlas is what establishes the industry.

Map:

- SMV capital providers and acquisition-first funds,
- patient-capital funds and permanent-capital vehicles,
- LMV and indie/AI-native micro-capital ecosystems,
- search / ETA investors and marketplaces,
- software acquirers and holding companies,
- sovereign-backed innovation capital,
- revenue-share, royalty, shared-earnings, milestone, and capped-return providers.

## Section 4: Strategic Middle Ventures

Purpose:

- Make SMVs the first deep proof case inside the atlas.
- Show that middle outcomes are not failed unicorn outcomes.

Subclaims:

- Strategic buyers value workflow ownership, proprietary data, customer depth, vertical depth, and integration value.
- PE and software platforms acquire durable, profitable software companies.
- Bootstrapped and delayed-VC companies can produce major outcomes.
- $10M-$500M exits and profitable independent companies can be economically meaningful even when they do not fit classic VC math.
- The first evidence should emphasize SMV firms and infrastructure, then use company outcomes as supporting examples.

Initial candidates:

- Wingify,
- ProfitWell,
- SocialPilot,
- FusionCharts,
- Habu,
- Appfire,
- Baremetrics,
- Drip,
- Grasshopper pending verification.

## Section 7: Liquidity As A Supporting Pain Point

Purpose:

- Explain that private middle-company value can be difficult to access, price, or sell.
- Treat liquidity as one reason the industry needs structure, not the central thesis.

Guardrail:

- The paper should not imply that private real estate fund redemptions directly finance Post-Unicorn Finance.
- Use examples like BREIT redemption terms only as liquidity-design analogies.
