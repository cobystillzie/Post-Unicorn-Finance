# VC-Backed Comparison Set (group = vc-backed)

All figures are sourced from SEC EDGAR filings (S-1 / S-1/A / 424B4). Figures are as-of each
filing date (IPO-era snapshots, 2020-2024); these firms are larger now. Headcount and capital
are internally consistent IPO-era snapshots, suitable for per-dollar comparison.

Voting power != economic ownership: dual-class structures concentrate VOTING control in
founders/insiders far above their economic stake. Where disclosed, economic concentration is
noted separately.

| Company | Sub-sector | Employees (as-of) | Concentration | Net IPO proceeds | Founded |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GitLab | DevOps / dev tools SaaS | ~1,350 team members (Jul 31 2021) | Class B = 99.1% voting; insiders + 5%+ holders = 62.3% voting | ~$610.6M | Inc. Sep 2014 (Delaware) |
| Snowflake | Data cloud / horizontal SaaS | 2,037 (Jul 31 2020) | Officers/dirs as group 33.8% economic; Sutter Hill 20.3%; Altimeter 14.8%; dual-class (Class B 10 votes) | not extracted (S-1 pricing blank) | 2012 |
| DoorDash | Food-delivery marketplace | 3,279 (Sep 30 2020) | 3 co-founders 69% voting (79% if all exercised); RSUs double-trigger (liquidity-event vesting) | ~$3.27B | Founded 2012 / inc. 2013 |
| Instacart (Maplebear) | Grocery-delivery marketplace | 3,486 full-time (Jun 30 2023) | Officers/dirs/5%+ ~42% of common (economic) | ~$563.8M (incl. concurrent PP) | 2012 |
| Affirm | Fintech / BNPL | 916 (Sep 30 2020) | Class B 93.7% voting; founder Levchin 19.8% voting | IPO $49/sh; net proceeds not stated in S-1/A reviewed | 2012 (op. entity) |
