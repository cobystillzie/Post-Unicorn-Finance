## A. Framing (confirm or correct)

1. **The comparative thesis.** We have framed the study around one testable claim: *does the post-unicorn
   model deliver more worker value-sharing — and better-distributed economic impact, per dollar deployed —
   than the unicorn-chasing VC model?* Is this the thesis you want, or should the descriptive "map of
   mechanisms" stay primary and the comparison secondary?
   *Default: comparative thesis is the spine; the catalog feeds it.*

2. **Orthogonal lens, not a new asset class.** We are treating value-sharing as a new lens that sits outside
   the INPUT→OUTPUT model — *not* as a new atlas asset class. Confirm that matches your intent (you called it
   "a side thing… how value is created… parameters on tipping the odds").
   *Default: lens, kept isolated from the atlas.*

## B. The fairness engine (confirm)

3. **Per-dollar + distribution as the fair basis,** with absolute impact reported alongside — does this
   resolve your concern that "it is hard to be a fund like SMV in the similar revenue category as a unicorn"?
   *Default: yes; per-dollar + distribution, absolute shown too.*

4. **Whole-portfolio denominator (failures counted).** We count the full fund portfolio including write-offs,
   not just survivors — this is the lever that stops VC's power-law from being flattered. Agreed?
   *Default: yes, whole portfolio.*

5. **Jobs created vs preserved; realized vs paper wealth.** We label jobs created (built-from-zero) separately
   from jobs preserved (acquired), and we lead with wealth that actually reached workers over paper grants.
   Agreed?
   *Default: yes to both.*

## C. Data and budget

   Licensed providers come *after* you see the 20-vs-20. The provider × metric × cost matrix is ready in
   `04_data_strategy.md`. **What budget, if any, can the licensed phase assume — and is there a preferred
   provider?**
   *Default: assume $0 until you approve a number; recommend the lowest-cost provider that covers headcount
   history + funding + ownership.*

7. **Scraping boundary.** Confirm we keep to compliant collection only (the project's "social = discovery
   only, don't bypass site protections" rule). The headcount idea (LinkedIn) is best served via a licensed
   provider or public filings rather than direct scraping.
   *Default: compliant sources only.*
